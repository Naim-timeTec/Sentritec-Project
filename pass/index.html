Perimeter rules

Prevent unauthorized remote unlocks with precise, administrator-defined security perimeters.

Two independent rules decide whether an app-based unlock is even possible. Apply either one, or require both.

Rule A — where the phone is

GPS Geofencing

Draw a boundary around the site. A remote unlock only goes through when the user's phone reports a location inside it — anywhere else, the command is refused before it reaches the door.

Rule B — what the phone is on

Wi-Fi Fencing

Bind the unlock to your own network. The phone has to be actively joined to an approved on-site SSID, so being near the building — or spoofing a location — isn't enough on its own.
